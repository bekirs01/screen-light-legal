What it does

Screen Light turns the display into a light while the app is open and in front. Color Light fills the screen with one color, from a preset or your own hue. Photo Light is a steady warm-to-cool white. Split Light puts two colors on one screen. Night Lamp is a dim warm light with a timer of 15, 30, or 60 minutes, or a custom length. Ambient Effects moves through candlelight, sunset, and a slow color flow. Moving Message shows large type, still or drifting. Favorites keeps color, photo, split, night, and ambient looks on this device.

Tap the light to hide the controls. Tap the light again to show them.

What it does not do

  • It is not the camera flash and it does not turn on an LED torch.
  • It stops when you leave for another app. It does not stay lit behind other apps.
  • It does not measure light, and it does not calibrate a studio lamp or a physical lamp. The App Store name, Screen Light: Night Lamp, describes this screen used as a nearby light.
  • Brightness and color depend on the screen you are holding. The app cannot make the panel brighter than the device allows, and it does not enable HDR. Phone light falls off quickly with distance. Hold the screen close.
  • One phone cannot be both a video call and this lamp. A call needs a second device if you want the light on yourself.
  • There is no account, no ad, and no in-app purchase screen. The app does not send favorites or settings to a server.

Brightness and wake

Brightness and keeping the screen awake apply only while Screen Light is in front. Keep screen awake can be turned off in Settings. That lets the phone sleep and does not change brightness. On iPhone and iPad, opening a light can change system brightness; the app tries to put the previous level back when the session ends. If the screen did not return to the brightness you expect, leave the light and open it again.

Favorites, motion, and the night lamp

Favorites, haptics, reduced motion, keep screen awake, and the night-lamp fade preference stay on the device. Opening a favorite restores the look only. It does not resume a timer. A finished night-lamp timer stays dark until you start it again. Moving Message text is not saved with a favorite.

The interface in this version is English. Settings does not include a language chooser.

No sound pack is installed, and Settings does not show a sound switch.

Reduce motion turns off motion inside Screen Light. If the device itself asks for reduced motion, that request wins and the switch stays on. Haptics can be turned off. Fade out timer dims a new Night Lamp before its timer ends. A saved favorite keeps its own fade.

Delete a favorite from the Favorites tab if you no longer want that look. Uninstalling removes the app’s private storage. There is no account to reset and no password.
